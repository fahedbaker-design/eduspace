# EduSpace

The offline teacher app of EduSpace (installable web app for iPad).
Course content and the students' pages stay online in Google Apps Script; this app keeps
course management and grading on the device and syncs when connected.

Current: 0.4 — linked to EduSpace through the panel's Sync service (read only: download the real courses; editing comes with two-way sync). Tips on round buttons. Demo data until linked. The first test page is test.html.
